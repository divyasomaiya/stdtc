Important Local Storage Notice: DTC-SilverTouch stores all transactions and payment proof screenshots locally inside your browser's IndexedDB. It does not sync automatically to any cloud or across different devices/domains (such as moving from local file:// to GitHub Pages). Download a Full JSON Backup regularly and keep your backup files private.
